## Central claim being mapped

The central contested premise is:

> **Group disparities in outcome are caused by injustice.**

The book examines what happens when institutions treat that causal interpretation as established fact rather than as a contestable causal claim.

A central distinction is:

* An observed disparity is a Category 1 observation.
* “The disparity is caused by discrimination/injustice” is a causal interpretation.
* The problem occurs when the causal interpretation is treated as though it were itself an observed fact.

Do not turn this into the stronger claim that the premise is necessarily false. The tool is showing what follows from treating it as settled.

## Important conceptual distinctions

Do not collapse these different kinds of relationships.

### Rival explanations

Biology, culture, individual preference, and behavior/agency are not a causal chain.

They are **parallel rival explanatory domains**.

The relevant logic is:

> If the disparity itself is treated as dispositive evidence of injustice, these alternative causes cannot receive independent explanatory weight without reopening the question of causal apportionment.

Do not create edges such as:

Culture → Behavior

unless the author-supplied source material explicitly supports such a relationship.

These rival explanations are primarily hidden reasoning infrastructure. They do not need to appear on the opening screen.

### Applied claims

Some recognizable downstream claims include:

* Gender is socially constructed.
* Pronoun recognition.
* Disparate impact.
* Land acknowledgments.
* Israel is committing genocide.

These are not all produced by the exact same intermediate reasoning. Preserve genuine differences in their routes.

### Rules of evidence

Two especially important concepts are structurally different from ordinary downstream claims:

* The powerful group's own account of its purpose—or a defensive purpose attributed to it—does not determine its responsibility or how the harm is classified. This does not mean intent disappears from the reasoning: the framework may discount an asserted, attributed, or presumed purpose while inferring a different intent from outcomes, patterns of harm, and the parties' relative positions.
* The social position / standpoint of affected people can have distinctive authority in identifying harm.

These rules help insulate the premise from revision.

### Power and directionality

Do not assume the premise is symmetric across all group disparities.

The author's framework has directionality: structural power helps determine whom the framework classifies as occupying the disadvantaged/victim position and whom it classifies as occupying the dominant/perpetrator position.

This is important for understanding why a disparity involving a historically or structurally subordinate group is treated differently from an apparently analogous disparity involving a dominant group.

The author's NBA-style counterexample is useful as a diagnostic, but it should not necessarily become a visible node.

Use the following author-approved sequence rather than presenting power as an unanswered question:

1. **Power lies with the group that benefits from—or is not harmed by—the injustice.**
2. **The framework treats the powerful group's position as produced or sustained by injustice.**
3. **Because its power rests on injustice, that group bears moral responsibility for harm to the less powerful group.**
4. **The powerful group's own account of its purpose—or a defensive purpose attributed to it—does not determine its responsibility or how the harm is classified.**

The map reconstructs the framework's reasoning rather than declaring that reasoning objectively true. Within that reconstruction, state the propositions directly. Preserve the distinction between identifying the group with power, explaining why that position carries responsibility, discounting the group's own or attributed purpose, and potentially inferring intent by other means.

### Israel is committing genocide

Do not omit this node.

It should be treated as a convergence case. The following is an author-approved reconstruction of the reasoning, not a declaration that its premises or conclusions are objectively true.

The power and legitimacy portion is:

1. **Israel is classified as the more powerful party because of its military and economic advantage and because it is often racialized as whiter.** "Whiter" describes a perception within the framework, not the actual racial identity of every Israeli or Israel's diverse population.
2. **Israel's establishment and territorial expansion dispossessed Palestinians.**
3. **Its military and economic power therefore rests on that territorial dispossession.**
4. **Its character as a Jewish state preserves unequal control or privilege.**
5. **Israel's position of power is therefore treated as produced and sustained by injustice.**
6. **The claim that Israel has no right to exist specifically as a Jewish state is a downstream judgment about legitimacy, not the explanation of why Israel has power.**

The portion showing how the premise shapes interpretation is:

1. **A self-defense rationale for Israel's actions may be asserted by Israel or attributed by others.**
2. **A self-defense rationale for Israel's actions does not determine how those actions are classified.**
3. **Harm to Palestinians is interpreted as evidence that Israel intends to eradicate Palestinians.**
4. **Power assignment and inferred intent together support the genocide classification.**
5. **Israel is committing genocide.**

The route therefore requires both the power/direction logic and the interpretive moves that become available once the central premise and power assignment are accepted.

For the visible route, use explicit referents rather than relying on pronouns or an unexplained jump from the central premise:

1. **The group benefiting from—or not harmed by—the injustice is classified as powerful.**
2. **Israel is classified as the powerful group.**
3. **Israel's power is rooted in injustice.**
4. **Israel bears moral responsibility because it is classified as the more powerful group.**

Keep the supporting qualifications in the node notes, but do not replace these explicit visible links with ambiguous wording such as **"its power."**

The tool should not imply that “Gender is socially constructed” causes “Israel is committing genocide.” Rather, when those two surface nodes are selected, the route should read from the selected left concept to the selected right concept while revealing shared architecture. Left-to-right arrows indicate reading order through the reconstruction, not causation between the two surface claims.

### Moral shadow

A key diagnostic supplied by the author is that the premise is functioning as settled when disagreement no longer registers as ordinary causal disagreement but as ignorance, denial, prejudice, or moral failure.

This is evidence about the epistemic status of the premise.

Do not simply represent “moral shadow” as an ordinary downstream policy claim. It is part of how the reader discovers that the premise has become settled.

## Interaction model

Opening state:

* The same surface concepts are visible in a left bank and a right bank.
* The right bank is unavailable until the reader chooses a first concept on the left.
* The central route field is empty.
* No explanatory edges.
* No visible hierarchy.
* No visible root.
* No visible analytical category labels.
* Only concise selection instructions; no explanatory prose about the argument.

Interaction:

1. User chooses the first concept from the left bank.
2. The same concept becomes disabled and faded in the right bank, preventing a concept from being paired with itself.
3. User chooses a different concept from the right bank.
4. The two selected surface concepts appear as endpoints in the central route field.
5. Reveal only the hidden reasoning nodes actually needed to connect them.
6. Keep unselected surface concepts in the side banks rather than fading them behind the revealed route.
7. Present revealed concepts as plain text stations rather than enclosed bubbles.
8. Arrange links to read consistently from left to right. This is traversal order, not a claim that the left surface concept causes the right surface concept.
9. The route should pass through the root where the argument actually requires it.
10. Reset restores both banks and clears the central route field.
11. Previously traversed routes may eventually leave a subtle trace.
12. After multiple traversals, the root should visibly accumulate weight.

On narrow screens, preserve the same sequence—first choice, second choice, then route—even if the banks and route must stack vertically.

Do not implement accumulation, animation, or complex transition effects until the conceptual routing is correct.

There should eventually be a “Show everything” option, but that is a secondary reveal mode, not the opening state.
